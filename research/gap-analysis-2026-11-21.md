## Priority 1 — 5G in Pakistan (biggest missing topic)
5G launched 19 March 2026 (Jazz + Zong; Ufone licensed, no consumer launch as
of late Oct 2026). 1,782 live sites across 24 cities (IT minister, 23 Oct
2026): Jazz 1,015, Zong 640, Ufone 127; ~1.8M users. Spectrum auction
10 Mar 2026: $507M, 480 MHz — Jazz 190 MHz, Ufone 180 MHz, Zong 110 MHz.
Cities: Karachi 385 sites, Lahore 373, Islamabad 276, Faisalabad 144,
Peshawar 122 + 19 more. Speeds: community median 5G ~220 Mbps (Jan–Jun
2026), another tracker ~130 Mbps; single-site tests Zong 244–290 Mbps,
Ufone up to 750 Mbps (F11 Islamabad). Phone check: band n78 (3.5 GHz)
essential, n41 (2.6 GHz) bonus. Dedicated competitor sites exist
(5gistan.com, 5ginpakistan.com, beam.pk).
Build: /5g hub guide (timeline, cities, operators, phone check) + Jazz 5G /
Zong 5G speed-test pages (or 5G sections on existing Jazz/Zong pages) +
Jazz vs Zong vs Ufone 5G comparison.

## Priority 4 — "Best ISP in Pakistan" hub
Major query; competitors techmag, nubiapage, PakSpeed, trending-pakistan all
have one. We have /compare + /statistics but no single ranked hub. Sources:
Ookla H1 2025 (Flash Fiber 56.95, Transworld 56.71, Nayatel 56.31),
Opensignal Apr 2026 (Nayatel Best Home Internet + Reliability award, 330
pts; PTCL ~60% fixed market share), PakSpeed 30-day (Nayatel 32.8,
Transworld 29.1, PTCL 21.7), nPerf 2025. Rank by speed + reliability + city
+ use case (gaming, WFH, budget). Note: PTCL closed PKR 108B purchase of
Telenor Pakistan in early 2026; fixed broadband ~3.7M subs mid-2026, fibre
~2.9M homes (vs <70k in 2018).

## Priority 5 — Missing PTCL pieces
- CharJi provider page: the 4th PTCL service; portable 4G LTE hotspot
  (CharJi EVO Cloud 36+ Mbps, up to 10 devices, 300GB FUP per third-party
  listings). EVO/Nitro are legacy. "PTCL CharJi speed test" is a real query.
- DSL vs Flash Fiber comparison page: ptclspeedtest.pk has a dedicated one;
  we cover it in packages/guides but have no dedicated page. Key facts:
  DSL asymmetric (1–1.5 Mbps up), distance-degraded, up to ~100 Mbps;
  Flash Fiber symmetric, up to 1 Gbps, now 100+ cities; PTCL expanding
  fibre fastest.
- PTCL vs StormFiber comparison: the two biggest providers — a surprising
  gap (we have vs Nayatel and vs Transworld).

## Priority 6 — Starlink in Pakistan status page
High interest, honest answer is "not available": SECP-registered
(Starlink Internet Services Pakistan), security clearance pending (Ministry
of Interior, confirmed to Senate committee 7 Nov 2026), no PSARB NOC, no
PTA licence, no official price or launch date. Six satellite firms
registered. Any PKR price online is an estimate — fits our no-invented-
prices stance. Needs dated updates when status changes.
